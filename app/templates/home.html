Every hire, one standard.

Rubric is a hiring platform for a private K-12 school built around "Who: The A Method for Hiring." It enforces a sequence of stage gates so that no one skips straight to a gut-feel decision -- and when someone needs to skip a gate for a good reason, the chairman can override it, but only with a reason that gets written to a permanent audit trail.

Scorecard

Decide what success in the role looks like before anyone is sourced.

Source

Build a pool of candidates systematically, not by waiting for applications to arrive.

Select

Run a structured sequence -- screening, Who interview, focused interviews, references -- that produces real evidence.

Sell

Once you've decided, make the case for why this candidate should say yes.

A 5-step tour

Each step switches you to the right role and takes you straight there. The demo data is seeded fresh -- if anything looks off, a chairman can reset the demo data.

  1. 1. Try to advance a candidate with only one reference

    As the principal, open Rahul Verma's application on the Maths opening and try to advance him. The gate panel will explain exactly what's missing.

  2. 2. Submit a rating and watch the others appear

    As interviewer Rohan Kapoor, submit the pending focused-interview rating for Priya Sharma (already hired, but this one rating was left open for the tour). Then open her application page: the other interviewers' ratings, hidden until now, will be visible.

  3. 3. Create an opening and draft its scorecard with AI

    As the principal, create a new opening, then use "Draft scorecard with AI" with a short brief. Review the draft before approving it -- that's the point.

  4. 4. Find the override on the dashboard

    As the chairman, open the dashboard's compliance panel and find the override on the Accounts Officer hire -- an internal transfer whose phone screening was waived, with the reason on record.

  5. 5. Read a full decision record

    Open Priya Sharma's decision record: the scorecard, every interview, every rating with its evidence, references, safeguarding checks, the decision, and the full audit trail -- what the chairman would read to explain any hire.
